About the Store

Why I started Vardhman NX

My mother used to take me along when she shopped for kurtis in Jabalpur, and I remember her walking out of shop after shop because nothing came in her size. Some shopkeepers even said, "Madam, this is not made for you." That sentence stayed with me.

In 2001 I opened Vardhman NX in Talaiya with one rule: no woman should be told her size is the problem. We began with fancy kurtis and matching sets, and slowly added plus sizes up to 6XL, because customers kept asking and we kept saying yes.

Today we sell to families who walk in from Bhaldarpura and to shopkeepers who buy wholesale from us. Both get the same honest rates and the same chai.

Shoury Jain, Owner
